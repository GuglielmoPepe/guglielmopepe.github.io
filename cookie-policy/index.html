Information about cookies and other tracking systems

What are cookies

Cookies are text files that websites visited by users send to their devices, where they are stored and then retransmitted to the same sites on the next visit. Cookies can be divided into two macro-categories: "technical cookies", which are necessary for navigation, and "profiling cookies", which are used to track users and send advertising.

This website does not use profiling cookies, third-party cookies, or any other active user tracking systems.

During navigation, no cookies are installed on the user's device. However, the technical hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) processes network technical data (such as server log files and IP addresses) for the sole purpose of ensuring the security, optimization, and protection of the service. The user's prior consent is not required for the processing of these technical data strictly necessary for security.
